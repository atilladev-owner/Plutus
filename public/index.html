Every fill is a transfer

Plutus is a multi asset ledger, and a paper trading exchange built directly on top of it. A transfer moves money between accounts as one or more legs, committed together under a database lock, never as application code hoping two writes do not race.

Every write is provable

Each write also appends one entry to a hash chained journal. The verify endpoint recomputes the whole chain on demand, so a stranger can confirm the books are honest without taking anyone's word for it.

A fill is a transfer

An exchange fill is a ledger transfer with three legs: the quote asset to the seller, a fee to the house, and the base asset to the buyer. The exchange cannot create or destroy money, and its own verify endpoint proves that on demand.

What to build with it

Accounts, transfers and holds are general enough to carry patterns well outside trading.

Savings groups

One ledger per group, one account per member. A contribution is a transfer into the shared pot. A payout on schedule is a transfer back out, and the journal is the record, not a spreadsheet.

Group expenses

An account per person, an expense recorded as a transfer between whoever paid and whoever owes. Who owes what is a balance read, not a reconciliation at the end of the trip.

Tabs

A hold reserves a ceiling when the tab opens, is captured for the exact amount owed at close, and releases the rest. Nobody's money moves until the tab is actually settled.

Deposits

A hold placed at booking, captured in full or in part if something was owed, released untouched if it never was. The hold's own state answers whether a deposit is still outstanding.

Three calls to start

No signup. A sandbox key comes back from one call, ready to use immediately.

01

Mint a key

curl -s -X POST https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/keys

02

Open a ledger

curl -s -X POST https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name":"shop"}'

03

Move money

curl -s -X POST https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/transfers \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: fund-1" \
  -d '{"legs":[{"from":"world:GHS","to":"'$TILL'","asset":"GHS","amount":"125000"}]}'
