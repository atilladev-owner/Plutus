What to build with it

Accounts, transfers and holds are general enough to carry patterns well outside trading. Four narratives, each with the calls that story needs and a small diagram of where the money goes.

Story 01

A savings group

Ama and five friends run a rotating savings group: everyone contributes on the first of the month, and one member takes the whole pot in turn. One ledger holds the group, one account per member plus a shared pot account. A contribution is a transfer into the pot. A payout on schedule is a transfer back out, and the journal is the record, not a notebook passed around a group chat.

curl -s -X POST \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/transfers \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: contribution-ama-sep" \
  -d '{"legs":[{"from":"'$AMA'","to":"'$POT'","asset":"GHS","amount":"5000"}]}'
curl -s -X POST \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/transfers \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: payout-kofi-sep" \
  -d '{"legs":[{"from":"'$POT'","to":"'$KOFI'","asset":"GHS","amount":"30000"}]}'

Story 02

Group expenses

Kofi and Ama share a flat and a ledger, one account each. Kofi pays for groceries and logs a transfer for Ama's share directly from her account to his. Nobody keeps a running spreadsheet: a balance read on either account settles who owes what, right up to the day they split the flat.

curl -s -X POST \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/transfers \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: groceries-week-1" \
  -d '{"legs":[{"from":"'$AMA'","to":"'$KOFI'","asset":"GHS","amount":"3000"}],"memo":"half of groceries"}'
curl -s \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/accounts/$KOFI \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY"

Story 03

A tab

A guest opens a bar tab. A hold reserves a ceiling against their account the moment it opens, so the till knows the money is there without taking it yet. At close, the hold is captured for the exact amount owed and the rest is released in the same call. Nobody's money moves until the tab is actually settled.

curl -s -X POST \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/holds \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"account":"'$GUEST'","amount":"20000","memo":"tab ceiling"}'
curl -s -X POST \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/holds/$HOLD/capture \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"to":"'$TILL'","amount":"14500","release_remainder":true}'

A tab's hold moves from opened to captured for what was owed, with the rest released in the same call.

Story 04

A deposit on a reservation

A hotel places a hold at booking, the size of the deposit policy. If the stay ends with nothing owed, the hold is released untouched. If there was a damage fee, the hold is captured for exactly that amount and the rest still goes back. The hold's own state answers whether a deposit is still outstanding, without a phone call to the front desk.

curl -s -X POST \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/holds \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"account":"'$GUEST'","amount":"50000","expires_in_seconds":604800,"memo":"reservation deposit"}'
curl -s -X POST \
  https://plutus-ten-eta.vercel.app/v1/ledgers/$LEDGER/holds/$HOLD/release \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY"
